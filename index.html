HOW TO DRIVE — FULL KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  1. Enter starts or stops the engine.
  2. Shift fully depresses the clutch immediately.
  3. Q presses clutch gradually; E releases clutch gradually, giving precise bite-point control without a slider.
  4. W / ↑ accelerator; S / ↓ brake; A / D steering.
  5. 1–6 choose gears; N neutral; R reverse.
  6. Launch: Enter → hold Shift → press 1 → release Shift → add W throttle → hold E to release clutch smoothly. Press Shift again if the engine is about to stall.
